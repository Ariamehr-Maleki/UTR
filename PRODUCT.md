# UTR — University of Tehran Research Hub

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Supabase Auth/Postgres/private Storage; Vercel. Selected to meet the requested stack and deployment constraints.

## Users and purpose

Persian-speaking students, approved instructors, Farabi research administrators, and technical superadmins. The umbrella platform covers research services, events, opportunities, journals, library resources and achievements. Initial institutional scope is the Farabi campus, not university-wide adoption.

## Core mechanism

An approved, attended event leads to one canonical handwritten summary and one active course/instructor referral. Each event earns 0–1 academic credit. Any positive score permanently locks student referral; zero permits another eligible instructor subject to configured deadlines. No aggregate semester or course cap. Actual grade entry remains governed by institutional policy.

## Brand commitments

Primary Persian RTL. Reference 1 controls blue/cyan/white identity. Supplied reference 2 is the authentic emblem; reference 3 is wing artwork. Exact font unidentified; Vazirmatn is explicitly temporary.

## Constraints

No invented events, names, endorsement, achievements, counts or testimonials. Demo records never enter public production content. Membership approval is distinct from email verification. Technical superadmin does not inherit academic authority. Unresolved policies disable affected behavior.

## Accessibility

Keyboard operation, visible focus, mobile document submission, readable Persian, isolated mixed-direction text, responsive 390/768/1440 layouts.
