# UTR design system
## Direction
An institutional research portal with a spacious typographic introduction, the supplied university emblem and a restrained turquoise wing watermark. Public pages persuade through clarity and real services; workspaces prioritize the next task. No fabricated activity.
## Tokens
University blue #153F7D; medium blue #086DC1; cyan #00B8CC; ink #080F2C; white #FFFFFF; pale blue #EDF8FC. Cyan is decorative; readable links/buttons use university blue. Destructive/error states use #A22336 with explicit text.
## Typography
Self-hosted Vazirmatn temporary fallback, weights 400/500/700/800. Exact reference font is pending user identification, never claimed matched. Body 16px/1.9; small 14px; section 28–36px; hero 44–64px with generous Persian line-height. English spans use bdi/dir=ltr.
## Layout
RTL, max width 1200px, 24px gutters (16px mobile), 8px spacing rhythm. Hero uses a 7:5 text/emblem split and collapses on mobile. Editorial rows and varied section scale prevent repetitive card grids. Workspace sidebar becomes wrapping navigation on small screens.
## Controls and states
44px minimum controls, associated labels, clear error recovery, native form semantics. Primary blue, secondary outlined, 12px panel corners. Focus ring 3px blue with white offset. Status always includes words. Tables scroll within their container; long names wrap. No decorative metrics or auto animations.
## Navigation
Public: platform, club, events, opportunities, resources, about, account. Workspaces: overview, submissions, courses, notifications, support, profile; instructor assessment; scoped administration. Role switching never grants permissions.
